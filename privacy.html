Privacy Policy – Wool Tree Idle

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Wool Tree Idle: Silk Tycoon (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed by Alauddin Ahmed ("we", "us"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect or store your personal information.

  • Game progress (coins, levels, items, settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the Game schedules reminders locally on your device. No data is sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, operating system and app usage information related to ads

This information is collected directly by Google, not by us. Please read how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent for personalized ads before they are shown. You can change your choice at any time.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver the item.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your choices

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to allow or stop tracking.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • You can turn notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • You can delete all Game data by uninstalling the Game.

6. Data security

Because game data stays on your device and we do not operate servers, we do not hold your personal data. Third-party services (Google, Apple) protect data according to their own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
